## Featured Case Study

### Kenangan Smart Pickup Time

Kenangan Smart Pickup Time is a product strategy case study focused on improving pickup-order confidence by addressing the gap between estimated and actual ready time during peak hours.

The case study covers:

- Product overview
- Problem framing
- Research findings
- Proposed solution
- Experimentation insight
- MVP requirements
- Key learnings
